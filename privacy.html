Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Pocket Blox ("the game") is made by Pixel Pocket Igre ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses on Android, iOS and the web version.

1. No account, no personal data from you

You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or location, and we do not collect them.

2. Game progress stays on your device

Your score, coins, furniture, Pixo's progress and settings are saved only on your device (local storage). We do not receive this data. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, the progress is deleted.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android and iOS apps show optional reward videos. You only see an ad if you choose to watch one. The ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process:

  • the advertising ID of your device,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • information about ad interactions (for example, whether a video was finished).

In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game. Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Web version

The web version shows no ads. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page is opened. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.), which may log technical access data such as your IP address for security reasons.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
